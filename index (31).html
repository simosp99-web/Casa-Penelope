OrtigiaThe island, a 5-minute walk

Siracusa's old town is an island joined by three bridges. Piazza Duomo, where the cathedral was built inside a Greek temple; the Fonte Aretusa with its papyrus; Castello Maniace at the tip; the Temple of Apollo just past the bridge.

Piazza DuomoFonte Aretusa Castello ManiaceTempio di Apollo
BeachesRocks in town, sand just outside

Cala Rossa and Arenella are the closest; Fontane Bianche has the sand and the beach clubs; the Plemmirio reserve is clear water over rock.

Which one to pick depending on the day's wind, and how to get there without a car: in the complete guide.
Ancient SiracusaNeapolis, the Greek theatre

The Neapolis Archaeological Park holds the Greek Theatre, the Ear of Dionysius and the Roman amphitheatre. In May and June the theatre still stages Greek tragedy.

Go early: after eleven there is no shade left.
